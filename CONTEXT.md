# Fudget

A household budget: money coming in, money going out, tracked for the household as a whole and for each person in it. Income is the base every other figure is measured against.

## People

**Household**:
Everyone sharing the budget, viewed as one unit.
_Avoid_: Family, joint, total

**Member**:
One person in the Household, with their own Income and Costs.
_Avoid_: Individual, person, user

## Money in

**Salary Package**:
A Member's yearly pay including employer super, as quoted by their employer.
_Avoid_: Gross, salary

**Take-home**:
What reaches a Member's bank after super, income tax, the low income tax offset, Medicare levy and HECS/HELP. A Member's Income in the budget is their Take-home.
_Avoid_: Net pay, after-tax

**Salary Sacrifice**:
Part of a Member's pay sent to super before tax, lowering Take-home but adding more to super.

**FHSS**:
First Home Super Saver: Salary Sacrifice that can later be released from super toward a first home deposit.

**Salary Packaging**:
Costs an employer pays from a Member's pay before tax, lowering taxable income. Exempt items (e.g. a laptop) don't count toward HELP; reportable ones (e.g. not-for-profit caps) do.

**Novated Lease**:
A car lease the employer pays from a Member's pay as Salary Packaging, with running costs bundled in. GST is claimed back; FBT is either exempt (electric cars under the luxury car threshold) or paid away from after-tax pay. Ends with a Residual.
_Avoid_: Car package, salary-packaged car

**Residual**:
The balloon owed at the end of a Novated Lease to keep the car, at least the ATO minimum for the term.
_Avoid_: Balloon payment (fine in UI copy), buyout

**Income**:
Money coming in. Belongs to one Member, or is Shared Income.
_Avoid_: Earnings, pay, salary

**Shared Income**:
Income that belongs to the Household but to no single Member, e.g. rent received or family benefits. Stays at Household level unless shared out to Members by a Split.
_Avoid_: Joint income, other income

**Household Income**:
All Members' Income plus Shared Income.

**Cycle**:
How often an amount recurs: weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly or yearly. Amounts on different Cycles are compared only after converting to monthly.
_Avoid_: Frequency, period

## Money out

**Cost**:
A recurring amount going out. Belongs to one Member, or is a Shared Cost.
_Avoid_: Expense, bill, outgoing

**Shared Cost**:
A Cost the Household pays together, divided between Members by a Split.
_Avoid_: Joint expense, household bill

**Split**:
How a Shared Cost or Shared Income is divided between Members: by Income share, equally, or not at all (left at Household level).

**Allocation**:
A monthly Household amount set aside after Costs, split like a Shared Cost. Spending Allocations count as Costs; saving ones are Savings Goals.
_Avoid_: Bucket, envelope

**Savings Goal**:
A target amount to put aside, measured against Income like a Cost.

**Joint Account**:
A bank account two or more Members save into together. Its balance is entered once, and its interest is taxed equally to each Member.
_Avoid_: Shared account, linked account

**Left Over**:
Income minus Costs and Savings Goals, for the Household or for one Member.
_Avoid_: Surplus, remaining, balance

## Planning

**Variation**:
One complete version of the budget's Costs, Allocations and settings, compared side by side with others.
_Avoid_: Scenario, version, budget copy

**Home Stage**:
Whether the Household is buying a first home (FHSS and the purchase count) or already owns one (its current mortgage and Offset balance count instead).

**Offset**:
Savings held against the mortgage so the balance in it attracts no interest, while staying available to withdraw.
