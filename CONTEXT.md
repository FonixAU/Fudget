# Fudget

A household budget: money coming in, money going out, tracked for the household as a whole and for each person in it.

## Language

**Household**:
Everyone sharing the budget, viewed as one unit.
_Avoid_: Family, joint, total

**Member**:
One person in the Household, with their own Income and spending.
_Avoid_: Individual, person, user

**Income**:
Money coming in, belonging to a Member or to the Household. It is the base that every Budget figure is measured against.
_Avoid_: Earnings, pay, salary
